🛡 Emergency controls

If the public forms are being misused (a flood of fake tickets, strange uploads), pause them. Visitors are told to e-mail support instead; requesters can still confirm and rate; staff are not affected. You will also receive a security e-mail.

⚠ Google Sites cannot pass the ticket number to a page, so it is not used for the e-mail buttons — they open the built-in site address instead (shown below).

📥 E-mail intake

Reads the support mailbox (read-only) and lists each new e-mail under E-mail Requests. Nothing becomes a ticket, and nobody is e-mailed, until you press “Make ticket”. First-time set-up: see EMAIL_INTAKE_GUIDE.md.

📌 Automatic assignment by category

New tickets in a category are assigned to the person you choose. They are e-mailed (if an e-mail is saved for them under Users) and the ticket shows their name. Anyone can still re-assign a ticket later. Only active users who can work on tickets are listed.

⚠ Reset Ticket Log (password protected)

Permanently clears all ticket records. Requires the reset password.
